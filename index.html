Our Story

The art
of two

Damian and Amira have spent over a decade refining a language spoken through movement. Trained in Buenos Aires and performing across four continents, their tango carries the weight of tradition and the breath of the contemporary.

Together they move as one — reading each other without words, navigating the floor with quiet authority. Their dance is not performance alone; it is a conversation between two people who have chosen to listen.
